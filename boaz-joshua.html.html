My name is Boaz Joshua. I'm a recent graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Valuation, and the founder and CEO of ZANMOVES — a premium travel startup redefining how the world experiences Tanzania.

I grew up watching the richness of this country — the coastlines of Zanzibar, the wilderness of the mainland, the warmth of the people — and I knew there was a gap between what Tanzania truly offers and how the world was experiencing it.

So I built something to close that gap. Not just a travel agency — a brand, a vision, a movement. ZANMOVES exists to give travelers something real: genuine local perspective, curated excellence, and experiences that stay with them long after they leave.

I'm passionate about entrepreneurship, branding, and the kind of business-building that actually creates meaning. Every day I'm learning, pushing, and working toward something I believe in deeply.

Founder Story

"The best founders don't wait for permission — they build the thing they wish existed."

The Beginning

A degree. A question. A decision.

Completing a degree in Valuation gave me structure, discipline, and a way of seeing value in the world — literally. But somewhere along the way, I started asking a different kind of question: What was Tanzania truly worth to the travelers who had never experienced her properly?

The Idea

Tanzania deserved better storytelling.

Tourism was happening — but the premium local experience, the insider routes, the genuine human connection — those were largely missing from what agencies were offering. I believed someone with deep roots here could do it differently.

The Build

ZANMOVES — from vision to reality.

I started building. Reaching out to hotels, learning the industry, developing the brand, and saying yes to every opportunity to grow. Within a relatively short period, ZANMOVES had formed partnerships with over 100 hotels across Zanzibar and mainland Tanzania, and was welcoming travelers from Switzerland, the UK, Austria, the US, and South Africa.

Now

Building for East Africa.

We're still early — and I say that proudly. What's been built so far is proof that the vision is real. The next chapter is about scale, brand, and expanding ZANMOVES into the leading premium travel company in East Africa.
